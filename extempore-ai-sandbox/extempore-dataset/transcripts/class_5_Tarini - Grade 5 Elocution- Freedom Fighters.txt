Good morning, friends and teachers. My name is Darni and today I will be talking about  Rani Lakshmi Vai, also known as Rani of Jhansi. Rani Lakshmi Vai was born in 19th November 1828  in the town of Varanasi into a Marhati Karhati Brahmin family. She was named Manika Nika Thambi  and was nicknamed Mano. Manika Nika was married to the Maharaja of Jhansi. Gangadar Rao Nivalka  in May 1842 and was afterwards called Lakshmi Pai in honor of the Hindu goddess Lakshmi and  according to the traditions. She gave birth to a boy later named Dhammudar Rao in 1851  who died after four months. The Maharaja adopted a child called Anandra, the son of Gangadar Rao's  cousin, who was named Renee and Dhammudar Rao on the day before the Maharaja died.  After all the British and Jhansi had been killed by Indian troops in June 1857,  the Rani took over the administration provisionally until the British returned.  She had to form an army to defeat the invading forces of Aukhya and Dantya. In March 1858,  British forces led by Sir Hugh Rose came to Jhansi to take back the city from the Rani who  now wanted independence. Jhansi was besieged and taken after strong resistance. Many of the people  of the city were killed in the fighting and afterwards. The Rani escaped the Kalki and Jhanshi  with the Maharaja general, Tatya Topey, then seized Gwalya. In the battle of Kothaki Sarai,  in which the army was defeated, Rani Lakshmi Vai was wounded and died on 18 June 1858.  As a allude to all the freedom fighters. Thank you.
