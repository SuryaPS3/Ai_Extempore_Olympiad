The COVID pandemic has terribly shook all the nations irrespective of size or strength. All sectors of economy are badly affected by the pandemic beyond human estimates. However, mankind has applied technology successfully to overcome the challenges thrown at us by the COVID virus. One of the most visible sectors is that of education where online teaching and learning has become the accepted rule. Subject experts from any corner of the world can be contacted online from any part of the world. Both parents and children have become tech savvy over the period of lockdown and are handling devices very well. Before the outbreak of COVID, going to clinics and hospitals to consult the doctor was the norm. But in the post-COVID scenario, telemedicines have gained prominence. Earlier, we used to go to different shops to buy different items. But with the advent of online shopping apps and home delivery channels, we can do our shopping comfortably from our home itself. Dining out, which is enjoyed by friends and families alike, is not allowed due to restrictions. But food delivery apps have brought our favorite restaurants to our doorsteps. Technology is revolving every passing day and the application of the same has brought about significant changes in the way we think, act, and communicate. Thank you.
