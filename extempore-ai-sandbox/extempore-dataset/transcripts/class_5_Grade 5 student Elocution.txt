Hello everyone. My name is Watson and today I'm going to speak about a very strong and dynamic freedom fighter of our country. Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, also known as the Iron Man of India. Vallabhbhai Jhaverbhai Patel, popularly known as Sardar Patel was born on 31st October 1875 in Nadiad village in Gujarat. He was a very brave child since his childhood. Sardar Patel became a law graduate and later traveled to England to become a barrister. After returning to India, he continued practicing law in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. A meeting with Mahatma Gandhi in October 1917 led him to join the Indian Independence struggle. He joined the Indian National Congress and his initial movements started with Satyagraha in Gujarat against British outrage. He actively participated and volunteered the Quit India movement in 1942, closely working with Gandhiji. He is one among the three leaders who led India from 1947 to 1950. Patel had a very strong contribution in uniting the people of India during India's freedom movements. During this time, he was jailed many times. The feeling of patriotism and urge to expel out the British from the Indian territory became his first and only objective. After Independence, his commitment to the national integration and his belief in the principle of unity in diversity earned him the title Iron Man of India. He persuaded the rulers of princely states to be united and be a part of One India One Nation. He was appointed as the first Home Minister and later the first Deputy Prime Minister of our country. Sardar Patel died on 15th December 1950 after suffering a massive heart attack in Bombay. He was conferred with the Bharat Ratna award posthumously in 1991. Rashtriya Ekta Diwas, National Unity Day was introduced by the Government of India in 2014 to pay tribute to Sardar Patel who was instrumental in keeping India united. The Statue of Unity, the world's tallest statue was dedicated to him on 31st October 2018. Thank you and have a very good day.
