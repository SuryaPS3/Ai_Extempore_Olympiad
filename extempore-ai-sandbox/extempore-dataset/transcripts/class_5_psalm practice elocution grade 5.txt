With Jesus came to Golgatha,
They hugged him on the tree.
They drove great nails through hands and feet
And made a calvary.
They crowned him with a crown of thorns.
Red were his wounds indeed,
For those were crude and cruel days
And human flesh was cheap.

When Jesus came to our town,
They simply passed him by.
They did not hurt or hear of him.
They did not watch him die.
For the men had grown more tender.
They would not cast a stone.
They simply passed him on the streets
And left him there alone.

Still Jesus cried,
"Forgive them,
For they know not what they do."
He prays this prayer for us tonight,
For you and you and you.
We,
we cruci-
We pass him by and leave him there
Without an eye to see,
And crucify our Lord afresh
As he prays for calvary.

Jesus on the cross is hung.
Oh, look, my soul and see!
The hands so oft outstretched in love
Are pinned to a tree.
Oh, brand my hands and body, Lord,
With marks of agony.

Jesus on the cross is hung.
How rich is mercy there!
A crown of glory laid aside,
A crown of thorns to wear.
When folks shall weave my thorny crown,
Each shame I'll gladly bear.
